# TrustVoice — Independent Oversight Without Unilateral Control

**One-sentence description:** TrustVoice enables protected whistleblowing and independent case review without giving any single administrator or organisation unilateral control over sensitive evidence or governed case decisions.

## 1. Vision & Originality (25%)

Most whistleblowing tools protect submissions. **TrustVoice focuses on what happens next:** investigating an organisation that may itself be implicated. Its innovation is **Conflict-Safe Independent Escalation**: a qualified outside reviewer can assess case-specific evidence under pre-established rules without taking unrestricted custody of the organisation's data.

## 2. Trust Constraint Insight (20%)

Reporters fear exposure or retaliation by conflicted insiders. Organisations fear exposing confidential files to outsiders. Reviewers need sufficient evidence to investigate. Ordinary administrator-controlled permissions may leave every party dependent on one authority. **The bottleneck is unilateral power, not merely weak encryption.**

## 3. Value & Impact (20%)

TrustVoice gives employees a safer reporting path, independent reviewers appropriately scoped access, and organisations a more accountable process. It also protects people named in reports through limited disclosure and fair-review principles. The initial use case concerns allegations involving a senior manager.

## 4. Why TideCloak Matters (20%)

TideCloak's **Emergent Authority** can make sensitive access contingent on identity, policy and required approvals rather than an application-held master key. TrustVoice aims to apply this to protected evidence and governed access changes. **Formal case closure requires a separately enforced workflow; TideCloak login alone does not protect database status.** TideCloak also does not guarantee investigator impartiality, data availability or protection against authorised recipients leaking information.

## 5. Prototype & Storytelling (15%)

**Planned synthetic-data demo:** An employee submits evidence. A compromised admin fails to decrypt it. A qualified reviewer successfully accesses only authorised case evidence. The interface explains how a governed review avoids unilateral control, with protected case outcomes shown only if independently enforced.
